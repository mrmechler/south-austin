What We Mean By South Austin

Not a scene. Not a skyline view. Just a really good zip code.

When this site says South Austin, it means 78745 and 78748 — plus the eastern edge of 78749, on the Austin side of MoPac. It does not mean 78704. It does not mean Zilker, Bouldin Creek, or Travis Heights, and it isn't Circle C either. Those are good places, and they get plenty of attention already. This is the other South Austin: the one that's still mostly single-story houses on quarter-acre lots, not listings with a skyline view.

It's close enough to downtown to matter — MoPac, I-35, and Ben White all get you there in one merge — and far enough out that you can still find a house with no HOA, a carport somebody turned into a studio, and oak trees older than the street sign. Nobody here is trying to convince you it's cool. It just works, and it has for a long time.

It's not a scene. It's a zip code that never needed to be one.

People end up here for the same handful of reasons: a yard big enough for a dog and a garden, a commute that doesn't run through downtown to get downtown, and a street where the houses don't all look like they were built the same week. Some are here for one of those. Most stay for all three.

Selling In South Austin

Selling here, honestly.

Austin's market has cooled from its 2022 peak, and more than half of active listings have taken at least one price cut this year. That's not a reason to panic — it's a reason to price correctly the first time. In a market like this, the home that's priced right on day one is the home that sells; the home that isn't sits, gets stale, and ends up selling for less anyway.

South Austin adds its own wrinkles: no two houses on the same street look alike, additions and converted garages aren't always permitted the same way, and buyers are comparing your 1975 ranch against brand-new builds in Buda and Kyle. Pricing an older South Austin home takes more than pulling three comps — it takes knowing which updates actually move the number and which just feel like they should.
