The South Side shortlist.

A few of the places people keep coming back to—for very different reasons.

Western Trails

Distinctive mid-century homes, larger lots, mature trees, and the kind of neighborhood character that cannot be manufactured.

Cherry Creek

Established, practical, well-connected, and full of streets that feel comfortably lived in.

Garrison Park

Relaxed, central, and easy to enjoy without needing the neighborhood to prove anything.

South Manchaca

Close-in energy, an eclectic housing mix, and quick access to the places people actually go.

Maple Run

Approachable homes, neighborhood parks, and a comfortable community feel that works for everyday life.

Olympic Heights

Newer construction, community amenities, and a more turnkey path into the South Austin lifestyle.
