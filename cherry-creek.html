Cherry Creek

Wide streets, real shade, and a lot of houses that don't match their neighbors. Cherry Creek is what people picture when they say "South Austin" and don't yet know there's a difference between this and 78704.

Westgate Blvd

Largely none in the original sections

What It Feels Like

Established, not manicured.

Cherry Creek reads as a neighborhood that got built once and then just got lived in. Streets are wide and shaded by trees that predate most of the houses under them. There's no design committee approving your paint color — in the original sections, there's no HOA at all — so the housing stock ranges from untouched 1970s ranch homes to full gut renovations, sometimes on the same block.

It isn't trying to be a "scene," and that's the appeal. Days here run on errands and routines more than reservations.

Housing Styles

No two houses quite alike.

Expect single-story mid-century ranch homes, a meaningful share of duplexes, and a growing number of updated remodels and modern infill builds. Lots tend to be generous by Austin standards, and the lack of HOA restrictions in the original sections means more freedom to add on, convert a garage, or park a second vehicle without a covenant getting in the way.

Who Tends To Appreciate It

Buyers who want room to make it theirs.

Cherry Creek tends to draw buyers who want a real yard, the freedom to renovate without an HOA weighing in, and a foothold in Austin proper without paying central-Austin prices. It's a strong fit for first-time buyers stretching for space and for move-up buyers who want a project with real upside.

Nearby

Parks, coffee, and the everyday stuff.

Outside: Mary Moore Searight Metropolitan Park (trails, disc golf), Dick Nichols District Park, Grey Rock Golf Club, and the Veloway for cyclists.

Coffee & food: Jo's Coffee on Menchaca, Pinthouse Pizza, and Bender Bar & Grill for a casual night out without leaving the neighborhood.

Honest Tradeoffs

Strengths, and what to weigh against them.

Strengths

  • No-HOA flexibility in the original sections
  • Mature trees and generous, shaded lots
  • Relative value versus 78704 and central Austin
  • Quick access to MoPac, I-35, and Ben White

Tradeoffs To Weigh

  • No HOA also means no shared amenities or upkeep standards
  • Older homes may need foundation, electrical, or HVAC updates
  • Housing styles vary block to block — visit in person
  • Some duplex-zoned pockets mixed among single-family streets
